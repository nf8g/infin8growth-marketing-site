## Homepage FAQ Content (5 Questions)

### Q1: How much does AI implementation cost for a small business?

The Foundation Install starts at $5,600 for teams of 1-10 people. That's a flat fee for a complete AI Operating System: your context captured, your tools connected, and 2-3 workflows running. Larger teams are scoped on a discovery call. After the install, ongoing expansion runs $1,500-4,500/month depending on whether you want us to maintain it, train your team, or stay embedded as a partner. We publish real numbers because you shouldn't have to book a call just to know if you can afford it.

---

### Q2: What do I actually get from a Foundation Install?

You get a working AI Operating System configured for your business. Specifically: a Context Layer (10-15 files capturing how your business works), a Connections Layer (your existing tools wired together through one interface), and a Workflows Layer (2-3 automated processes running by the end of week two). You also get your team trained to use it. This isn't a strategy document or a recommendations report. It's infrastructure you operate the day we hand it over.

---

### Q3: How long does the Foundation Install take?

About two weeks. Week one is building your Context Layer: a guided session where we capture how your business actually runs. Week two is connecting your tools and configuring your first workflows. By the end of day ten, you have a working system. This isn't a multi-month consulting engagement. It's an install with a defined timeline, defined deliverables, and a working system at the end.

---

### Q4: How is this different from other AI investments I could make?

Most AI investments are tools or training. This is infrastructure. A tool solves one problem and sits alongside everything else. Training teaches concepts but doesn't change how work gets done. An AI Operating System becomes the layer your business runs on. It compounds over time because every workflow we add builds on the foundation we've already installed. You're not buying a subscription. You're building an asset that gets more valuable the longer it runs.

---

### Q5: We've tried AI before and it didn't stick. Why would this be different?

Because we build the foundation before the features. Most AI experiments fail because they start with a tool instead of starting with context. The tool doesn't know your business, so it gives generic outputs. Your team gets frustrated. The tool gets shelved. We start with your business knowledge captured and your systems connected. When AI has that foundation, it stops feeling like an experiment and starts feeling like infrastructure. That's why it sticks.

## Full FAQ Page Content (All 12 Questions)

### Section 1: Getting Started

**Q1: Where do I start with AI for my business?**

Start with your foundation, not your tools. Most businesses jump straight to buying AI software before they've captured how their business actually works. That's backwards. The first step is documenting your context: who you are, what you do, how decisions get made, and where time gets lost. Once that's in place, AI has something to work with. Without it, you're just adding another tool to the pile.

---

**Q2: Do we need to fix our data and processes before AI will work?**

You don't need perfect data. You need captured knowledge. Most businesses think they need a massive data cleanup project before AI can help. That's not true. What you need is your institutional knowledge written down in a way AI can reference: your processes, your priorities, your decision-making patterns. We build that with you in the first week. The data cleanup happens naturally as the system starts running, because now you can see where the gaps are.

---

**Q3: What's the difference between AI tools and an AI Operating System?**

AI tools solve one problem at a time. An AI Operating System connects everything. A tool might help you write emails or schedule posts. An Operating System knows your business context, connects to your existing software, and runs workflows that actually match how your team works. Tools sit on top of your business. An Operating System becomes the layer your business runs on. The difference is whether AI is an add-on or infrastructure.

---

### Section 2: Cost & Value

**Q4: How much does AI implementation cost for a small business?**

The Foundation Install starts at $5,600 for teams of 1-10 people. That's a flat fee for a complete AI Operating System: your context captured, your tools connected, and 2-3 workflows running. After the install, ongoing expansion runs $1,500-4,500/month depending on whether you want us to maintain it, train your team, or stay embedded as a partner. We publish real numbers because you shouldn't have to book a call just to know if you can afford it.

**Q5: How do I measure ROI from AI?**

Measure time recovered, not outputs generated. The question isn't "how many emails did AI write?" It's "how many hours did my team get back, and what did they do with them?" We track hours saved on specific workflows, reduction in manual handoffs, and speed improvements on recurring tasks. Most clients see measurable results within 60-90 days. If you can't point to time recovered or errors reduced, the AI isn't working.

---

### Section 3: Working With Us

**Q7: How long does the Foundation Install take?**

About two weeks. Week one is building your Context Layer: a guided session where we capture how your business actually runs. Week two is connecting your tools and configuring your first workflows. By the end of day ten, you have a working system. This isn't a multi-month consulting engagement. It's an install with a defined timeline, defined deliverables, and a working system at the end.

---

**Q8: Do I need to learn new software?**

You'll use Claude as your primary interface, which works like a conversation. There's no complex dashboard to master. You ask questions in plain English, and the system responds using your business context. If your team can send a text message, they can use this. The learning curve is days, not months. We handle the technical configuration so you can focus on using it, not building it.

---

**Q9: Can my team maintain the system after you leave?**

Yes, and that's the point. We build AI capability, not dependency. After the Foundation Install, your team knows how to use the system, update the context files, and request new workflows. Some clients run it themselves from there. Others stay on a monthly retainer where we maintain it, add new capabilities, or train additional team members. Either way, you own the system. It runs on your accounts, with your data, under your control.

---

**Q10: What happens if AI makes a mistake?**

Someone on your team catches it before it reaches a customer. We design workflows with human checkpoints on anything that's customer-facing or involves money. AI drafts, routes, and prepares. A person approves before it goes out. The goal isn't full automation. It's removing the repetitive work so your team can focus on the decisions that actually matter. Mistakes happen when AI runs unsupervised. We don't build unsupervised systems.

---
