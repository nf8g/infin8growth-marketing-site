## The Main Point

Here's where you make your main argument. Use clear, direct language. Break up long sections with subheadings.

> [!quote]
> This is a pull quote. Use it to highlight a key insight or memorable phrase from your writing.

Continue with more content after the pull quote. Explain, elaborate, give examples.

## A Practical Example

When you want to highlight a key takeaway or actionable insight, use a callout box:

> [!callout title="Key Takeaway"]
> This is the one thing you want readers to remember. Make it specific and actionable.

### Going Deeper

You can use h3 headings for subsections within a larger topic. Use **bold text** for emphasis and *italics* for technical terms or book titles.

Lists work too:

- First point
- Second point
- Third point

## Wrapping Up

End with a clear conclusion. What should the reader do next? What should they think about?

The best Field Notes issues leave readers with something practical they can try this week.
